Your first visit

What to expect, step by step

  1. 01

    We listen

    Fifteen unhurried minutes to talk about what brings you in, what worries you and what you would like.

  2. 02

    We look, and explain

    A careful examination, with X-rays only if they are needed. Everything we see is shown and explained on screen.

    You decide

    You leave with clear options, honest costs and time to think. Nothing is booked until you are ready.
