Smile design & veneers

About this treatment

Smile design starts with your face, not your teeth. We plan proportions and shade together, preview the result with you, and then choose the gentlest way to get there: whitening, bonding or thin porcelain veneers.

How it works

Step by step

  1. 01

    Conversation

    What you would like to change, and what you would like to keep.

  2. 02

    Preview

    A mock-up of the planned smile, so you can see it before any treatment.

  3. 03

    Treatment

    Veneers or bonding made to the agreed design, fitted over one or two visits.
