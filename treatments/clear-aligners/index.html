About this treatment

Clear aligners are thin, removable trays that move your teeth a little at a time. You see the planned result before you begin.

How it works

Step by step

  1. 01

    Assessment

    We check whether aligners suit your teeth and bite.

  2. 02

    Your plan

    A digital plan showing how your teeth will move.

  3. 03

    Wearing them

    A new set every week or two, with short reviews.

  4. 04

    Keeping it

    Retainers hold your teeth in their new place.
