Dental implants

A fixed replacement for a missing tooth, planned around the tooth you will see.

Book a visit

About this treatment

An implant is a small titanium post that takes the place of a missing tooth’s root. Once it has settled into the jaw, a crown, bridge or denture is fixed on top. We plan the visible tooth first, then place the implant to support it.

How it works

Step by step

  1. 01

    Consultation

    An examination, the scans needed to plan safely, and a clear explanation of your options.

  2. 02

    Placement

    The implant is placed under local anaesthetic, at a pace that keeps you comfortable.

  3. 03

    Healing

    The bone bonds with the implant over the following weeks. We review how it is settling.

  4. 04

    Your new tooth

    A crown made to match your smile, fitted with your bite checked carefully.

Good to know

It depends on your gums, your bone and your general health. A consultation is the only way to know.

Usually a few months from placement to the final tooth, with healing time in between. You will get a personal timeline.

With good home care and regular reviews, implants can last for many years.
