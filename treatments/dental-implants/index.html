Dental implants

A fixed tooth to replace a missing one.

Book a visit

About this treatment

An implant is a small titanium post that replaces a missing tooth’s root. Once it has settled, a crown is fixed on top that looks and works like your own tooth.

How it works

Step by step

  1. 01

    Consultation

    An examination and the scans needed to plan safely.

  2. 02

    Placement

    The implant is placed under local anaesthetic.

  3. 03

    Healing

    A few weeks for the bone to bond with the implant.

  4. 04

    Your new tooth

    A crown matched to your smile, with your bite checked.
